## How long does a good taper last?

About 2 weeks. Bosquet and colleagues (2007) analysed 27 studies with competitive athletes. The best effect on performance came from a 2-week taper. A newer meta-analysis in endurance athletes (Wang and colleagues, 2023) found an effect for tapers of up to 21 days.

There is no taper study for HYROX itself yet. The advice here comes from endurance sport, team sport and strength sport. For HYROX, with running and heavy stations, those together are the best there is. A meta-analysis in team sports (Vachon and colleagues, 2021) found more maximal power and a higher VO₂max after a taper. Those are sports that, like HYROX, combine endurance and strength.

## What do you change in your training?

Mostly the volume. Intensity and the number of sessions stay as equal as possible.

| Item | What research shows | How Benchmrk does it |
|---|---|---|
| Length of the taper | 2 weeks worked best | 2 weeks |
| Volume | 41 to 60% less worked best | About 65%, then 40% of your volume |
| Intensity | Keep it the same | Short sections at race pace stay in |
| Number of sessions | Keep it the same or at most about 20% fewer | Short easy runs instead of extra rest days |
| Pattern | Gradual reduction works better than in one step | Volume drops further in race week |

Mujika and Padilla (2003) describe a taper as a gradual, non-linear reduction of training load. According to their review, a taper usually yields about 3%, with a range of 0.5 to 6%.

## When do you do your last hard sim?

10 to 14 days before the race. That is a Benchmrk rule, not the result of a study. After that last hard sim comes a short, sharp version: about half the volume, at the same race pace. In the taper, that is for example 4 × (500 m + station at 30% of race volume). How a sim grows towards that last dress rehearsal is covered in [build a HYROX simulation](/en/guide/build-a-hyrox-simulation).

Wang and colleagues found that a taper gives more after a block with extra load. The peak phase with the full dress rehearsal is that block.

## When do you stop heavy strength?

7 to 10 days before the race. After that you only do light station technique, nothing to failure. This is also a rule of the app, derived from literature on peaking in strength sport. A review by Travis and colleagues (2020) does note that there is little hard data on exactly how to peak maximal strength. In the last heavy session, the app keeps the same weight, with half the sets.

## What does race week look like?

Short, easy and with a few sharp sections. An example of a race week in Benchmrk, for a race on Saturday:

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Sun | Short easy run with 4 × 20 s at race pace |
| Tue | 4 × 400 m, 15 s/km faster than your threshold pace |
| Wed | Light station technique |
| Thu | Rest |
| Fri | 20 min shake-out with 4 × 20 s at race pace, or rest |
| Sat | Race |

The long run, the intervals and the sim become short, easy runs in race week. That way you keep the number of sessions about equal while the volume drops a lot.

## What do you eat before and during the race?

Make sure you are well fuelled at the start. HYROX cites an average race time of about 90 minutes; in the study by Brandt and colleagues (2025), the median finish time was 86.5 minutes. According to a review by Burke and colleagues (2011), for intense effort of about an hour even small amounts of carbohydrate help, even just rinsing with a sports drink. For longer effort, 30 to 60 grams per hour is a suitable goal. Always test what you eat on race day in a training session first.

## Where does it often go wrong?

You often see three mistakes:

1. **Too much rest.** In the meta-analysis a taper worked best when intensity and the number of sessions stayed the same. So keep short sections at race pace.
2. **Another hard sim.** A full dress rehearsal in the last week doesn't fit a volume that should be 41 to 60% lower.
3. **Starting too fast.** HYROX itself warns that many athletes run their first kilometre too hard. You set your race pace in advance, see [run pacing](/en/guide/hyrox-run-pacing).

How the taper fits in the whole plan is on [HYROX training plan](/en/hyrox-training-plan). How many weeks you need in total is covered in [how many weeks to train for HYROX](/en/guide/how-many-weeks-to-train-for-hyrox).

## How Benchmrk does it

The taper is in every plan of 8 weeks or longer by default. The app lowers your volume to about 65% and then 40%, plans your last hard sim 10 to 14 days before the race and your last heavy strength 7 to 10 days before. In race week, long sessions become short runs with sections at race pace. On race day you get pacing and nutrition advice.
