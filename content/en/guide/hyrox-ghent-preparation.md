## When is HYROX Ghent and what do you need to arrange?

HYROX Ghent takes place from 17 to 20 December 2026 at Flanders Expo, Maaltekouter 1. The schedule per division is provisional. You get your personal start time about 3 days beforehand, and it cannot be changed.

| What | According to the race page |
|---|---|
| Dates | Thursday 17 to Sunday 20 December 2026 |
| Location | Flanders Expo, Ghent |
| Doors | 07:00 for all participants |
| Early check-in | Wednesday 16 December, 17:00 to 20:00, only for those racing on Thursday |
| Check-in on race day | From 90 minutes before the first start wave of your division |
| Cancelling | Only with Flex Add-On: 100% back up to 31 days before the race (16 November), 50% up to 7 days before |
| Transfers | Not possible, not even to another division |

Check the terms of your own ticket. You cannot add the Flex Add-On afterwards.

## How do you divide 8 weeks?

6 weeks of training and 2 weeks of taper. This is an example split for a race on Thursday 17 December, meant as a starting point, not a fixed rule of the app.

| Week | Period | Phase | Core |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 22 to 28 Oct | Starting point | Threshold test, easy running weeks, strength for your weakest stations |
| 2 and 3 | 29 Oct to 11 Nov | Threshold | Threshold intervals, a sim at about half of race volume |
| 4 and 5 | 12 to 25 Nov | Compromised running | Running at race pace after station work |
| 6 | 26 Nov to 2 Dec | Peak | Sim at race weights, recalibrate paces |
| 7 | 3 to 9 Dec | Taper 1 | About 65% volume; last heavy sim between 3 and 7 Dec |
| 8 | 10 to 16 Dec | Taper 2 | About 40% volume; last heavy strength between 7 and 10 Dec |

What the full 16-week build looks like is on [the HYROX training plan](/en/hyrox-training-plan). With 8 weeks the phases get shorter.

## What do you do in the six training weeks?

You train running under fatigue. In the study by Brandt and colleagues (2025), 11 recreational athletes ran a simulated race. The median finish time was 86.5 minutes, of which a median of 51.2 minutes was running. According to the researchers that was more than 60% of race time. VO₂max was most strongly linked to finish time; grip strength and muscle mass were not.

The researchers did not measure running under fatigue. So compromised running is a Benchmrk choice based on the research, not a proven biggest factor. See [building a HYROX simulation](/en/guide/build-a-hyrox-simulation) and [compromised running](/en/compromised-running).

An example week for the compromised phase (5 days):

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Threshold: 4 × 1600 m at threshold pace minus 3 s/km |
| Tue | Station strength for your two weakest stations |
| Thu | Easy run, below 89% of your threshold heart rate |
| Sat | Sim: 8 × (500 m + station at 50% of race volume) |
| Sun | Long run, easy |

## How do you taper for Ghent?

Taper for 2 weeks with less volume and the same pace. Bosquet and colleagues (2007) analysed 27 studies; a taper of 2 weeks worked best, with 41 to 60% less volume. Benchmrk uses about 65% in the first and 40% in the second taper week. Those are rules of the app. If you race later than Thursday, shift all the dates. More in [the HYROX taper](/en/guide/hyrox-taper).

## How do you adjust this if you race later in Ghent?

Shift the taper and your last sim, and keep the training weeks the same. If you race as a man on Saturday 19 December, your taper starts on 5 December and your last heavy sim falls between 5 and 9 December. If that gives you 2 extra days, use them for an easy run or a light station session, not for an extra heavy session.

You cannot change your division after you enter. So look at the weights now, see [Open or Pro](/en/guide/hyrox-open-vs-pro).

## How Benchmrk does it

You enter your race date and division, and the plan counts back from that day. With 8 weeks the phases get shorter and the 2-week taper stays. The app plans your last heavy sim 10 to 14 days before the race, uses your own threshold pace and adjusts the plan to what you log. With the [quick check](/#check) you see beforehand whether your goal time fits.
